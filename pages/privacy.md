## Information collected

### Newsletter subscriptions

If you subscribe via the newsletter form, {{ metadata.author.name }} collects your name and email address through [Loops](https://loops.so/privacy), the email service used to send letters. Subscriptions use double opt-in — you'll get a confirmation email before you're actually added to the list.

### Website analytics

This site uses [Umami](https://umami.is/), a self-hosted, privacy-first analytics tool that:

- Does not use cookies
- Does not collect personally identifying information
- Does not store your raw IP address — it's used only to approximate your country, then discarded
- Runs on a server {{ metadata.author.name }} controls directly — analytics data is never sent to a third-party analytics company

Collected data includes page views, referring site, approximate location (country level), device/browser type, and — if you arrived via a tagged link — campaign source information (UTM parameters). This data is aggregate and isn't tied back to you individually.

### Webmentions

This site accepts [webmentions](https://webmention.io/) — a way for other sites to register that they've linked to or replied to a letter. If your site sends one, the mention (your site's URL and the linked content) is displayed publicly alongside the letter it references.

## How this information is used

- Sending the newsletter to subscribers
- Understanding which letters and pages get read, to inform what gets written next
- Basic security and abuse prevention

## Third-party services

Two third-party services are involved in running this site:

- **Loops:** newsletter delivery and subscriber management
- **Webmention.io:** receiving and relaying webmentions from other sites

Website analytics (Umami) are **not** a third-party service — they run on infrastructure {{ metadata.author.name }} operates directly. See "Website analytics" above.

## Your rights

You can:

- Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, via the link in any email
- Request a copy of the data held about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data

To exercise any of these, email [{{ metadata.author.email }}](mailto:{{ metadata.author.email }}).

## Data retention

- **Newsletter subscribers:** until you unsubscribe or request deletion
- **Analytics data:** retained indefinitely on the self-hosted instance unless deleted on request — since it isn't tied to an identifiable person, there's no per-person record to expire

## Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing is based on your consent (newsletter subscription, given via double opt-in) and legitimate interest (understanding site usage to improve the content). If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

## Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them.
