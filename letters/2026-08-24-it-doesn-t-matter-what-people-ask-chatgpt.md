> *"It doesn't really matter anymore what questions someone is asking ChatGPT — it matters what questions ChatGPT is asking your website after that happens, because of how query fanout works. You can ask one question, but it's broken up into 40, 50, 60 questions you never even see."*

Name query fanout as the mechanism and make it the centerpiece: one question a user types gets broken into 40, 50, sometimes 60 sub-questions your content has to answer, invisibly, before an AI engine will cite you. Use the "3,400 questions" audit example as concrete proof this is measurable, not hand-wavy — Squiz generates that many questions against a single 12-page topic on its own corporate site to find the gaps.
