I'm not sure about you, but I am so tired of talking about AI search. But there's just so much misinformation floating around, so many different products you could buy, so many salespeople. And no one wants to sit through another demo.

Do you even know what works for AI search citations? If not, that makes sense. Most of the process is opaque, and most of the SaaS products promising to show you  how many times your institution or organization shows up in AI search answers are light on why that matters, if it matters at all.

So first off, if it's a branded search — just like in SEO — you're probably going to get cited or at least included in the research. Second, in most cases that person searching on ChatGPT or Google has used it before. They're probably logged in. Just like when you use those apps for anything, it remembers context. So answers are going to be swayed by whatever other research they're doing. Searches still don't happen in a vacuum.

I said this on a live podcast in July 2026:

> *"It doesn't really matter anymore what questions someone is asking ChatGPT — it matters what questions ChatGPT is asking your website after that happens. Because of how query fanout works, you can ask one question, but it's broken up into 40, 50, 60 questions you never even see."*

That's the real truth. AI search platforms don't really answer that one question
